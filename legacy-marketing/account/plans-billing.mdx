## Free Trial

Spyro AI offers a **3-day free trial** so you can experience automated search and citation growth before committing. During the trial you get:
* **500 free credits**
* Up to **1 full article** generated and published
* Real-time site health audit and AI readiness analysis
* Cancel anytime during the trial with no questions asked

## How Credits Work

Every plan draws from a single organization-level credit pool shared across all your workspaces:
* **Blog article (researched, written, with AI images, auto internal/external links)**: 250 credits
* **Spyro AI chat answer / Ask Spyro AI**: 10 credits
* **AI citation check**: 10 credits per prompt × engine (Claude: 20 credits)
* **Keyword research search**: 10 credits
* **Keyword analysis view**: 20 credits
* **Competitor research scan**: 150 credits per competitor domain
* **LLM mentions scan**: 70 credits per tracked competitor (monthly)
* **Rank check**: 2 credits per tracked keyword
* **Internal linking scan**: 20 credits per verified fix
* **Additional workspace**: 300 credits (first workspace is free)
* **Site audits and SERP lookups**: **Free (0 credits)**

## How Billing Works

Billing is securely processed through **Dodo Payments** as the Merchant of Record, handling all global VAT, GST, and sales tax compliance:
* **Monthly subscriptions** renew automatically every month on your signup date.
* **Annual subscriptions** renew once a year with two months free included.
* Manage your subscription, payment cards, and invoices anytime under **Settings → Plan & billing**.
