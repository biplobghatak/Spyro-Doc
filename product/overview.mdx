You don't need to be technical to use it. Spyro AI checks your website, tracks your rankings, tells you whether AI tools mention your brand, and even writes and publishes content for you on autopilot. This guide explains every feature in plain language, step by step.

## How credits work

Paid actions in Spyro AI — like generating articles, running competitor research scans, and performing deep keyword analysis — draw from your organization's monthly credit pool:

* **Starter plans**: Include **4,000 credits / month** (up to 2 workspaces).
* **Pro plans**: Include **15,000 credits / month** (up to 10 workspaces).
* **Agency plans**: Include **80,000 credits / month** (unlimited workspaces).

Credits are shared seamlessly across all your workspaces. Site audits, crawls, and basic SERP lookups are always free. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage) for the complete breakdown of plan pricing and credit costs.

## Who Spyro AI is for

Spyro AI is built for anyone who wants more organic traffic and AI search visibility without the technical headache:

* **Founders and business owners** looking for automated search visibility
* **SEO professionals and marketing agencies** managing multiple client domains
* **Growth and marketing teams** scaling content and citation share
* **Content writers and editors** producing citable, high-ranking articles

If you can use a web browser, you can use Spyro AI.

## How Spyro AI is organized

A quick bit of vocabulary that makes everything else easy:

* A **workspace** represents one website you want to grow. Everything — your audits, rankings, content calendar, and settings — belongs to a workspace.
* If you manage multiple websites or clients, you can create a dedicated workspace for each and switch between them at the top of the dashboard.

## Get started in minutes

1. **Create your account and add your website**: Sign up and enter your domain. Spyro AI analyzes your site to understand your brand and competitors. See [Set up your account](/product/onboarding).
2. **Run your first audit**: Evaluate your site's health and review prioritized fixes. See [Run your first audit](/product/audit).
3. **Monitor your AI visibility**: Track whether leading AI models cite your brand in relevant prompts. See [AI visibility & competitors](/product/ai-visibility).
4. **Create content that ranks**: Use the [content calendar](/product/calendar) and [AI writer](/product/writer) to produce and publish articles that get cited and rank.
