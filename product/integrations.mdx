Integrations let Spyro AI work with the tools you already use. There are two kinds:

1. **Publishing connections** - so Spyro AI can send finished articles straight to your website.
2. **Data connections** - so Spyro AI can pull in your real search performance.

You don't need any coding to set these up.

## Publish your articles

Connect where your blog lives, and you can publish articles from Spyro AI with one click. You can set these up under **Content settings → Integrations**.

> **WordPress (self-hosted)**
>
> For a WordPress site you host yourself.

> **Shopify**
>
> Push articles straight to your Shopify blog.

> **Webhooks**
>
> For sending your articles to any other system that accepts them.

### Connect WordPress (self-hosted)

**1. Create an Application Password in WordPress**

In your WordPress admin, go to your user profile and create a new **Application Password**. This is a special password just for Spyro AI - safer than your real one.

**2. Add it to Spyro AI**

In Spyro AI, open **Content settings → Integrations**, choose **WordPress (self-hosted)**, and paste your site address, username, and the application password.

**3. Publish**

Now, when you finish an article in the [AI writer](/product/writer), you can publish it straight to your site.

### Connect Shopify

**1. Enter your Shopify store address**

In Spyro AI, open **Content settings → Integrations**, choose **Shopify**, and enter your store address (for example, `my-store.myshopify.com`).

**2. Connect with Shopify**

Click **Continue with Shopify** and approve the permissions. Spyro AI publishes articles straight to your Shopify blog via the Admin API.

**3. Publish**

Now, when you finish an article in the [AI writer](/product/writer), you can publish it straight to your Shopify store.

### Connect a Webhook

Choose **Webhooks** and paste the web address (URL) that should receive your articles. Spyro AI will send each published article there. This is handy if your site runs on a different system - ask whoever built your site for the right URL.

> [!TIP]
>
> After connecting, each integration shows its status (connected, paused, or having trouble) and a history of what was sent, so you can confirm everything is working.

## Connect your search data

Connect **Google Search Console** to bring your real Google search performance into Spyro AI - clicks, impressions, and the pages and searches that bring you visitors. See [Google Search Console](/product/search-console) for the simple sign-in steps.

You can also connect **Google Analytics 4** to track AI-driven referral traffic - visitors who reached your site from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and other AI engines.

## API keys (for advanced setups)

If you use other software that needs to talk to Spyro AI directly, you can create an **API key** - a secret code that connects them. You'll find this under **Settings → API**. Most people never need this, so feel free to skip it.
