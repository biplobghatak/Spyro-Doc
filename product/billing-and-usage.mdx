Spyro AI uses a simple credit-based model: every plan includes a monthly credit pool that powers paid actions like writing articles, running citation checks, and analyzing competitor research. This page explains our plans (including Starter, Pro, and Agency with monthly and yearly options), how credits work, and how to manage your billing.

## Start with a free trial

New accounts begin with a **3-day free trial** that includes **500 credits** and a quota of **1 free article generation** — enough to take the AI writer, citation tracking, and audit tools for a spin. You can cancel at any time during your trial with no questions asked. When the trial ends, your chosen plan activates to keep your automated growth running.

<Note>
  Unused trial credits are cleared when you subscribe to a paid plan.
</Note>

## Credits — how they work

Every plan includes one **org-wide monthly credit pool** shared across all your workspaces. Paid actions draw directly from this pool, which means margin and usage are completely uniform without juggling rigid per-feature limits.

* **Fair exchange rate**: 1 credit = $0.001 in computing resources ($1.00 = 1,000 credits).
* **Shared pool**: All workspaces within your organization draw from the same credit balance.
* **Monthly reset**: Credits refresh each month on your billing cycle date.

## Where to see your credit balance

You can monitor your credit consumption and quota in real time:

* **Sidebar**: The bottom of the dashboard sidebar displays your credits used versus your monthly limit. Hover over the meter to see the shared pool status.
* **Billing settings**: Navigate to **Settings → Plan & billing** to view your exact credit consumption for the current cycle, your next billing reset date, and invoices.

<Note>
  During your 3-day free trial, your sidebar displays the 500-credit trial pool and 1-article allowance instead of regular monthly plan limits.
</Note>

## Running out of credits

If an action requires more credits than your remaining monthly balance, Spyro AI will notify you and indicate how many credits are needed. You can:
1. **Upgrade your plan** anytime to instantly unlock a larger monthly credit pool (e.g. Starter → Pro, or Pro → Agency).
2. **Wait for your monthly billing reset**, when your full plan credit allowance refreshes automatically.
3. **Contact sales** for custom high-volume credit arrangements if your organization exceeds the Agency tier.

## Upgrading or changing your plan

1. **Open Plan & billing**: In the dashboard, click **Settings** and select **Plan & billing**.
2. **Select your plan**: Choose between Starter, Pro, or Agency, and select **Monthly** or **Yearly** billing.
3. **Checkout securely**: Payment is processed through Dodo Payments (Merchant of Record), ensuring complete regional tax and compliance handling.
4. **Instant upgrade**: Your new credit allowance and workspace limits take effect immediately, with prorated billing applied automatically.

<Note>
  Subscriptions cover your entire organization. Only organization owners and administrators have permission to modify plans or payment methods.
</Note>
