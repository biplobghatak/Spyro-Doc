## Getting started

> **Do I need to be technical to use Spyro AI?**
>
> No. Spyro AI is built for founders, marketers, and business owners. If you can use a web browser, you can use Spyro AI. The only step is optionally connecting your CMS (WordPress, Shopify, Webflow) to publish articles, and we guide you step-by-step in [Integrations](/product/integrations).

> **Is there a free trial?**
>
> Yes. New accounts get a **3-day free trial** that includes **500 credits** and **1 free article generation**, so you can try the AI writer, citation tracking, and audits before committing. You can cancel anytime during the trial with no questions asked. Unused trial credits are cleared when you subscribe.

> **How do I add my website?**
>
> You add it when you sign up. Spyro AI takes a quick look at your site to learn your brand, audience, and competitors. See [Set up your account](/product/onboarding).

> **Can I track more than one website?**
>
> Yes. Each website lives in its own **workspace**.
> * **Starter**: Up to 2 workspaces
> * **Pro**: Up to 10 workspaces
> * **Agency**: Unlimited workspaces
> See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).

## Plans and pricing

> **How much does Spyro AI cost?**
>
> * **Starter**: $29/month (or $288/year — save 17%) for up to 2 workspaces and 4,000 credits/month.
> * **Pro (Recommended)**: $99/month (or $990/year — 2 months free) for up to 10 workspaces and 15,000 credits/month.
> * **Agency**: $399/month (or $3,990/year — 2 months free) for unlimited workspaces and 80,000 credits/month.
> 
> Annual plans save you up to 20% compared to monthly billing. Check out [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage) for full details.

> **How many articles can I write each month?**
>
> Generating a complete, 2,500+ word SEO & GEO-optimized article with AI-generated images costs **250 credits**.
> * Starter includes 4,000 credits/month (up to 8 articles/month)
> * Pro includes 15,000 credits/month (up to 30 articles/month)
> * Agency includes 80,000 credits/month (up to 120 articles/month)
> 
> The 3-day free trial includes 1 free article generation. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).

> **How many keywords can I track?**
>
> You can track active keywords per workspace with weekly rank checks on Google:
> * **Starter**: Up to 100 keywords per workspace
> * **Pro**: Up to 500 keywords per workspace
> * **Agency**: Up to 1,500 keywords per workspace
> See [Rank tracker](/product/rank-tracker).

> **Can I add my team?**
>
> Yes. All plans include **unlimited team members**. Invite teammates and assign roles. See [Team members](/product/team-members).

> **What are credits?**
>
> Credits are the unified currency Spyro AI uses for paid actions like writing articles, running competitor research scans, and tracking AI citations. Every organization has one shared credit pool across all workspaces:
> * **Starter**: 4,000 credits/month
> * **Pro**: 15,000 credits/month
> * **Agency**: 80,000 credits/month

> **What uses credits?**
>
> * Blog article generation: **250 credits**
> * Spyro AI agent / Ask Spyro AI answer: **10 credits**
> * AI citation check: **10 credits** per prompt × engine (Claude: 20 credits)
> * Keyword research search: **10 credits**
> * Keyword analysis view: **20 credits**
> * Rank check: **2 credits** per keyword
> * Competitor research scan: **150 credits** per competitor domain
> * LLM mentions scan: **70 credits** per tracked competitor (monthly)
> * Internal linking scan: **20 credits** per verified fix
> * Additional workspace: **300 credits** (first workspace is free)
> * Site audits and crawls: **Free (0 credits)**
> * SERP lookups: **Free (0 credits)**

> **When do my credits reset?**
>
> Credits reset automatically every month on your billing anniversary date.

> **What happens when I run out of credits?**
>
> Paid actions pause until you upgrade your plan or your monthly credit pool refreshes on your next billing date. For higher volumes beyond Agency, contact us for a Custom plan.

## Features

> **Which AI assistants does Spyro AI track?**
>
> * **Pro and Agency**: Track all 5 major AI engines — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
> * **Starter**: Tracks ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews.
> See [AI visibility & competitors](/product/ai-visibility).

> **How does Spyro AI know if AI assistants mention me?**
>
> Spyro AI queries AI assistants with the natural buyer questions your target customers ask, then analyzes the responses to verify if your brand or domain is cited or recommended. Because generative AI answers vary, checks run on a weekly schedule to map citation trends and share of voice against competitors.

> **Can Spyro AI publish to my website?**
>
> Yes. You can connect **WordPress (self-hosted)**, **Shopify**, **Webflow**, or a **webhook**, and publish SEO/GEO-optimized articles with one click. See [Integrations](/product/integrations).

> **Does Spyro AI write the whole article for me?**
>
> Yes. The [AI writer](/product/writer) researches search intent and competitor angles, generates comprehensive 2,500+ word drafts with answer-first hooks, embeds relevant AI-generated illustrations, adds schema markup, and handles internal/external linking.

> **Can I connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics?**
>
> Yes. You can connect **Google Search Console** to pull your real search queries, clicks, and impressions, and **Google Analytics 4** to track AI-driven referral traffic. See [Integrations](/product/integrations).

## What to expect

> **How long does an audit take?**
>
> Usually one to two minutes. Larger websites may take slightly longer as Spyro AI evaluates pages for technical SEO and AI crawler accessibility.

> **How often does Spyro AI check my rankings and AI mentions?**
>
> Spyro AI monitors **AI citations** and **Google keyword rankings** every week. When you add a new keyword or publish a fresh article, it performs an immediate check.

> **My scores aren't perfect — is that bad?**
>
> Not at all. Prioritize the high-impact warnings and errors flagged in your report, re-test with on-demand URL checks, and watch your Site Health and AI Search Health climb over time. See [Understand your reports](/product/understanding-reports).

> **How do I change or cancel my plan?**
>
> Open **Settings → Plan & billing**. You can upgrade, switch between monthly and yearly billing, or manage cancellation anytime. There are no contracts or hidden cancellation fees. See [Plans & billing](/product/billing-and-usage).
