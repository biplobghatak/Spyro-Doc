**Content settings** is where you tell Spyro AI how you want your articles to sound and look. Set these once and every article you create will match your brand - the right voice, the right style, and images in a look you choose. You also connect the website where Spyro AI publishes for you.

## Why use it

* **Stay on-brand.** Every article uses your voice, your colors, and a consistent image style.
* **Set it and forget it.** Choose your preferences once and they apply to all future articles.
* **Publish automatically.** Connect your website and let Spyro AI post for you.

## What you can set

**1. Brand voice and tone**

Spyro AI learns a voice from your own website - its summary, tone, audience, and how formal it sounds - so articles read like you, not a generic robot. You can refresh it any time to match your latest content.

**2. Article style**

Pick the default writing style for new articles.

**3. Image style**

Choose one of several preset looks for your article images - for example flat vector, hand-drawn ink, isometric 3D, editorial photo, minimal geometric, risograph, paper cut, or technical blueprint. Every image is created in that style.

**4. Brand colors**

Spyro AI picks up your brand colors from your website and uses them to tint your images and accents. Adjust them if you'd like.

**5. Auto-publish**

Turn this on and finished articles publish to your connected website automatically, instead of waiting for you to push them.

**6. Publishing connections**

Connect where you want Spyro AI to publish (see below).

## Where you can publish

Spyro AI can publish your finished articles to:

* **WordPress (self-hosted)** - your own WordPress website.
* **Shopify** - push articles straight to your Shopify blog.
* **Webhooks** - send your article to another system using a webhook URL.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Wix** and **Framer** are shown as **coming soon** and are not available yet. The options you can connect today are WordPress (self-hosted), Shopify, and Webhooks.

To set up any of these connections, see [Integrations](/product/integrations).

## What to expect

Once your voice, style, images, and colors are set, you don't have to think about them again - every new article follows them. Each publishing connection shows whether it's connected and a short history of recent posts, so you can see at a glance that everything is working.

## Tips

* Set your voice and image style **before** you write your first article so it comes out right the first time.
* Refresh your brand voice whenever your website changes meaningfully.
* Turn on auto-publish only once you're happy with how your articles look - until then, review each one before it goes live.
