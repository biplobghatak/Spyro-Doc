Setting up Spyro AI takes just a few minutes. You create an account, tell Spyro AI your website address, and Spyro AI takes a quick look at your site to learn your brand, your audience, and your competitors. When you're done, you land on your **dashboard** - your home base for everything else.

A **workspace** is simply one website you want to grow. Everything you do in Spyro AI - audits, rankings, content, and settings - lives inside a workspace. Setup creates your first one.

## Why this matters

The few questions Spyro AI asks during setup help it work smarter for you later. Once Spyro AI knows your brand, who you sell to, and who you compete with, it can write better content, ask the right questions to AI assistants, and give you fixes that actually fit your site.

## Sign up

![Sign Up](https://mintcdn.com/spyro/kihzlJFiUqdSDslA/images/Sign-up.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=kihzlJFiUqdSDslA\&q=85\&s=48e09e712ce8220b31238f17f1c47089)

**1. Create your account**

Open the sign-up page and start your free trial. Enter your name, email, and a password - or choose **Continue with Google** to skip the password step.

**2. Confirm your email**

Spyro AI emails you a short verification code. Type it in to confirm your address. If it doesn't arrive, click **Resend code**. (If you signed up with Google, you can skip this step.)

**3. Add your website**

On the first setup screen, enter your website address (for example, `example.com`) and choose **Analyze**. Spyro AI starts learning about your site right away.

> [!TIP]
>
> Already have an account? Just use the **Log in** page instead, with your email and password (or **Continue with Google**).

## What Spyro AI does automatically

The moment you add your website, Spyro AI takes a quick look at it and pre-fills much of your setup for you. Anything it figures out shows up with an **Auto-detected from your website** label, so you know it came from your site.

Spyro AI automatically works out:

* Your **brand name, brand color, logo, and language**
* Your **audience** - who your site is for, and a short description of your business
* Your **competitors** - other sites going after the same customers
* A starter set of **AI-visibility questions** - the buyer questions Spyro AI will ask AI assistants to see if they mention you

> [!NOTE]
>
> Spyro AI only reads your **public** pages - the same ones any visitor can see. Everything it fills in is a **draft you can edit**. Change anything you like, and Spyro AI won't overwrite your edits.

## Finish the setup

Spyro AI walks you through a short, friendly wizard so you can review and tweak what it found. You can move forward and back, or jump to any step:

> **Business profile**
>
> Confirm the basics - brand name, color, logo, language, and country. Your country helps Spyro AI find the right competitors for your region.

> **Your audience**
>
> Review who your site is for and your business description. Keep at least one audience group to continue.

> **Competitors**
>
> Review the rivals Spyro AI found. Add or remove any. If the list looks off, click **Retry** to search again.

> **Blog setup**
>
> Point Spyro AI at your existing content to learn from - your blog and a few of your best articles. You can also connect Google Search Console here to bring in real search data.

> **Article style**
>
> Set the defaults for content Spyro AI writes for you - tone, focus, publishing pace, and featured-image style. You can change these later.

> **AI visibility questions**
>
> Review the buyer questions Spyro AI will ask AI assistants every week. Edit them to match how your customers really search for what you offer.

> **A couple of quick questions**
>
> Spyro AI asks whether you'd like a hand setting up, and how you found Spyro AI. Then choose **Finish setup**.

## Landing in your workspace

When you choose **Finish setup**, Spyro AI takes you to your dashboard and starts the heavy lifting in the background - looking over your whole site, running your first audit, and putting together a starter content plan.

You'll see a setup banner and a content-plan progress note while that work runs. The cards on your dashboard fill in on their own as the results come in - no need to refresh.

> [!TIP]
>
> If you close the tab partway through, just reopen Spyro AI. It picks up right where you left off, and finished workspaces drop you straight on the dashboard.
