A **tracking prompt** is a real buyer question - the kind a customer would actually type into an AI assistant, like *"best budget standing desk for a home office."* Every week, Spyro AI asks the AI assistants your prompts and records how often the answers mention you.

Prompts are the starting point for [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility): the questions you set here are what everything else measures. The trick is to ask the way a customer would - never your own brand name - because the whole point is to see whether AI recommends you on its own.

## Why use it

> **Ask like a buyer**
>
> Track the real questions your customers ask AI before they buy.

> **Choose your assistants**
>
> Decide which AI assistants Spyro AI checks for each question.

> **See who you're up against**
>
> Each prompt shows the competitor sites the assistants mention.

> **Follow the trend**
>
> Watch how often you're mentioned for each question over time.

## You start with suggestions

You don't begin with a blank page. When you first set up Spyro AI during [onboarding](/product/onboarding), it suggests a starter set of buyer questions based on your website and competitors. They cover a natural mix - recommendations, comparisons, and how-to questions - and you can review or edit them before they go live.

## How to add and manage prompts

**1. Open Tracking prompts**

In the sidebar, open **Tracking prompts** (part of AI visibility).

**2. Write a buyer question**

Use the **Add prompt** box and phrase it the way a real customer would - for example, `best budget standing desk for a home office`. Don't include your own brand name.

**3. Choose the AI assistants**

Tick which assistants should answer this question - **ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews**. They're all selected by default.

**4. Set the frequency**

Choose **daily** or **weekly** for how often Spyro AI re-checks this question. Daily gives you faster trend data; weekly uses fewer credits.

**5. Track it**

Click **Track prompt**. It runs once right away so you get a first result, then re-checks automatically at your chosen frequency.

To change a question later, edit it from its card. You can **pause** a prompt to stop checking it, or **delete** it to remove it for good. (Deleting also erases that prompt's history, so pause instead if you might want it back.)

## Reading your results

Each prompt appears as a card showing how it's doing this week:

* **How often you're mentioned** - your mention rate for that question this week.
* **The trend** - whether you're up or down compared with last week.
* **Per-assistant results** - how you did on each AI assistant you picked.
* **Competitor sites** - the rival domains the assistants mentioned for this question. This is a quick way to see who you're competing against.

Open any prompt to see its full history - how each assistant's results have moved over time, your top competitor for that question, and the most recent answers that mentioned you.

## What to expect

* Results are shown as a frequency, like **"mentioned in 3 of 5 checks,"** because AI answers vary each time.
* Your first result appears the moment you add a prompt; after that, Spyro AI re-checks automatically at your chosen frequency (daily or weekly).
* The trend needs a couple of weeks of data before it means much.

## Tips

* Keep prompts in your customer's words. If it sounds like an SEO keyword, rewrite it as a natural question.
* Use the competitor sites on each card to spot rivals worth studying over on [AI visibility](/product/ai-visibility).
* To read the exact wording of an answer, jump to [AI mentions](/product/ai-mentions).
